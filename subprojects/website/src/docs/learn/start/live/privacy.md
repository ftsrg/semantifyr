# Privacy

[Semantifyr Live](https://live.semantifyr.org) verifies your model on its server. The server stores your model, and the files its verifications write, only while your session is open, and deletes them when the session ends.

Semantifyr Live is a research prototype provided as is. Its authors take no responsibility for the models you upload; do not upload confidential models. To verify a confidential model, run Semantifyr on your own machine, in [VS Code](../vscode.md) or on the [command line](../command-line.mdx).
