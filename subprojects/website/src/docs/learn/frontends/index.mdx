# Frontends

A frontend brings an engineering language to Semantifyr: its translation into OXSTS, its semantic library and its traces. Semantifyr is a general framework, and SysML v2 and Gamma are its currently implemented frontends, for a modeller who brings a model in either language. Each has a part of its own, with what of the language it supports, what it assumes of the language's meaning, its command line and the form of its traces.

- [SysML v2](sysml-v2/index.mdx): parts, ports, items and state machines, with verification cases whose objectives are requirements, and traces written as SysML v2 models.
- [Gamma](gamma/index.mdx): statecharts and their composition in Gamma's syntax, with verification cases in the model, and traces in Gamma's trace language.
