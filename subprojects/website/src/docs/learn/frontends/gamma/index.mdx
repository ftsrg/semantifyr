# Gamma

The Gamma frontend verifies models of [Gamma](https://github.com/ftsrg/gamma), the statechart composition language of the BME [Critical Systems Research Group](https://ftsrg.mit.bme.hu/en/). It translates a model and its verification cases, written in Gamma's syntax, into a Semantifyr model on the Gamma semantic library.

- [Verifying Gamma Models](verifying.mdx): verify a model in Live and on your machine.
- [Lesson: A Level Crossing](lesson.md): read and verify a small model step by step.
- [Language Support](language-support.mdx): the models we support.
- [Semantic Decisions](semantic-decisions.mdx): the semantics we give them.
- [Traces](traces.mdx): how execution traces are represented in Gamma.
