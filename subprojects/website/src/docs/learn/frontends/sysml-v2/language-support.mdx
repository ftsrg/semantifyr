# SysML v2 Language Support

The SysML v2 frontend supports the part of the language that describes behaviour at the level of states: part and port definitions with their usages, items sent over flows between ports, attributes of type Integer and Boolean, exhibited state machines, and verification cases whose objectives are requirements over a part.

The table lists every SysML v2 construct the frontend reads, with its outcome. A planned or unsupported construct is reported on the construct, and the model is not translated. Every other construct is translated unless its detail says otherwise, and one with a warning says what to watch for.
