# What of SysML v2 Is Supported

The SysML v2 frontend takes the part of the language that describes behaviour at the level of states: part and port definitions with their usages, items sent over flows between ports, attributes of type Integer and Boolean, exhibited state machines, and verification cases whose objectives are requirements over a part. What the frontend assumes of the meaning of these constructs is on [Semantic Assumptions](assumptions.md).

Every SysML v2 construct the translator reads, generated from its registry with its outcome: a planned or an unsupported construct is a diagnostic located on it and no OXSTS is written; every other one is accepted and translated unless its detail says it is not, and one with a warning draws a warning that says what the model should know.
